## Group G (not tested by the judge)

- Bit rot in the middle of the file: every record has a CRC32-C, so a flipped bit is detected (`VerifyRecord`), but recovery cannot tell it from a torn tail: `scanLog` stops at the first bad CRC and `truncateTail` cuts everything after it, acked records included. `TestRecoveryStopsAtBadCRC` shows this. Known weakness, see BREAKING.md section 3.
- Network access, multiple files, sharding: out of scope.
- Multi-batch transactions, isolation levels, MVCC: out of scope.
- Performance beyond the 2 s recovery limit: out of scope.
